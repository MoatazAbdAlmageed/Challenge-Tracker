Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Challenge Tracker. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension handles information.

2. Information Collection

Challenge Tracker does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

The extension works entirely locally within your browser. All challenge names, dates, and progress data are stored using the chrome.storage.local API. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us or any third party.

3. Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions to function:

  • Storage: Used to save your challenges and progress locally on your computer.

4. Third-Party Services

Challenge Tracker does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services. There are no cookies or tracking scripts involved in the operation of the extension.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our GitHub repository or official support channels.
